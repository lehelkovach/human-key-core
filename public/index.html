QuickBooks Payments CSV Preauthorization

Upload a comma-delimited CSV. Cells are handled as text: cell 1 card number, cell 2 CVV, cell 3 expiration text, and cell 6 ZIP code. Cells 4 and 5 are reserved.

The requested expiration format was written as "mm/ds"; this app displays the value and only enforces mm/yy or mm/yyyy when live QuickBooks calls are enabled.
